FAQ

Good to know

Answers before you get started.

Does it work without internet?

Yes. The games work offline. Nearby multiplayer uses Wi-Fi and Bluetooth, without internet or accounts.

Do I need Apple Intelligence?

No. Board, arcade and classic games work without it. AI cards, coaching and drawing features require a compatible device and supported language.

Is there anything to pay for?

No. The app is free, with no ads or in-app purchases.

Where is my data kept?

Favourites, scores and settings stay on your device. AI runs on the device; camera-game photos are not saved or uploaded.
