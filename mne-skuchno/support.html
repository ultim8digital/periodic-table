A little help, whenever you need it

Let's get you back to playing.

Questions about I'm Bored: Offline Games? Email ultim8digital@gmail.com. Please include your app version, device model and the steps that led to the problem.

Which devices can I use?

The current app requires iOS or iPadOS 26 or later. Check its App Store page for the current device requirements.

Do the games need internet or Apple Intelligence?

Board, arcade and classic games work offline without Apple Intelligence. AI features need a compatible device, enabled Apple Intelligence and a supported language. Camera games need iOS 27.

How do I play on several nearby phones?

One phone hosts and the others join over Wi-Fi and Bluetooth. No internet or accounts are required.

What should I send to support?

Your app version, device model, chosen language and clear steps. Remove personal information from screenshots. Do not send sensitive information or photographs from camera games.
