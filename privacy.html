PERIODIC TABLE

Privacy policy

Last updated: 11 September 2026

Periodic Table is an offline educational reference published by Oleg Korunov. This policy describes the mobile app distributed through Google Play and the App Store.

App data

The app does not create accounts, collect or transmit personal information, or include advertising, analytics or tracking SDKs. It does not request access to your contacts, camera, microphone or location.

Settings and searches

Your selected language and theme are stored on your device. Searches and filter choices are processed locally and are not sent to a server. Search history is not saved. You can change your settings in the app. Removing the app removes its local settings; operating-system restore behavior is controlled by the platform.

Offline reference

The element data, translations and fonts are included in the app. No internet connection is required to browse the installed mobile reference. This policy does not cover third-party websites you choose to open from source addresses. Those websites have their own privacy practices.

Support

If you choose to email us, we receive the information you include and use it to respond to your request. Please do not send sensitive information. Email is handled by your email provider and ours; this is separate from using the offline app.

Policy changes

Any change to the app’s data practices will be reflected here with an updated date.
