PERIODIC TABLE

Privacy policy

Last updated: 23 October 2026

Periodic Table is an educational chemistry app with an interactive periodic table and a chemistry handbook, published by Oleg Korunov. This policy describes the mobile app distributed through Google Play and the App Store. The same policy is available inside the app, under About → Privacy.

No accounts, no tracking

The app does not create accounts and contains no advertising, analytics or tracking code. It has no servers of its own and does not send your data anywhere.

What is stored on your device

Only your language, theme, starred articles, practice results, the free practice counter and whether you bought Periodic Table Pro are saved on the device. Formulas and numbers you type into the solvers, your questions to the tutor and your search text stay in memory while the app runs and are never sent anywhere.

The handbook works offline

Articles, tables, translations and fonts are installed with the app. Search, the solvers, practice and the tables work without a network connection.

AI tutor (Periodic Table Pro)

The tutor uses Apple’s on-device Apple Intelligence language model. Your questions and the answers are processed on your iPhone or iPad and are not sent to us or to any third-party server. The tutor is not available on Android.

Camera and photos (Periodic Table Pro)

The camera is used only by the problem scanner and is requested the first time you scan. You can also pick a photo from your library through the system picker — the app receives only the image you choose. Text is recognised on the device; images are not saved and are not sent anywhere. The app does not use the microphone, contacts or location.

Purchases

Periodic Table Pro is an auto-renewable subscription or a one-time in-app purchase processed by the App Store or Google Play under their own terms. We never receive your payment details or your identity; the app only remembers on the device that Pro is unlocked.

Stores and the operating system

Google Play, the App Store and your operating system handle installation and update data under their own rules. That happens outside the app, and we do not receive that data.

Children

The app is suitable for students and collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Update checks

From version 2.0.2, the iOS app checks the public App Store listing for a newer version. Apple receives a normal HTTPS request and the IP address needed to deliver it. Questions, photos, formulas, searches, study progress and purchase history are not included. The check date and your choice to postpone an update are saved only on your device. A failed check never blocks the offline reference.

Deleting your data

To delete what is saved, clear the app data or uninstall the app. A Periodic Table Pro purchase can be restored at any time with “Restore purchases”.

Contacting us

If you write to us by email, we receive your address and the contents of your message and use them only to reply. Please do not send passwords or other sensitive information.

Policy changes

Any change to the app’s data practices will be reflected here with an updated date.
